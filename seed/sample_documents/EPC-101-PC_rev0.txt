PARTICULAR CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT - EPC-101 (fictional)

These Particular Conditions amend the General Conditions.

8.4 Notice of Delay
Clause 8.4 of the General Conditions is amended so that the Contractor shall give notice within ten (10) days after becoming aware of any event likely to delay completion.

14.5 Payment of Retention
One half of the retention shall be released on taking-over and the balance on expiry of the defects liability period.
